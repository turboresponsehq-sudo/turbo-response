# Creator Automations — Rapper Landing Page Template v1

**Status:** Reusable starter template. Derived from the approved 4L G5IVE website layout. Artist information and copyrighted imagery removed.

## Reuse for a new rapper
1. Copy the entire \`templates/rapper-landing-v1\` directory into a fresh project or branch.
2. Open **\`artist.config.js\`**. Change artist name, neighborhood, crew, location, bio, booking email, color, music/video IDs, and social URLs. **No HTML changes needed for standard customization.**
3. Add five images in an \`images/\` folder:
   - \`hero.jpg\`: wide or portrait professional artist image; show chains/faces, avoid placing text over jewelry.
   - \`neighborhood.jpg\`: place or neighborhood roots.
   - \`release.jpg\`: featured song photo/cover.
   - \`interview.jpg\`: interview photo.
   - \`story.jpg\`: alternate artist or community image.
   You can use full HTTPS image URLs instead of local files. In the template, missing photos show a stylized background.
4. Get the **11-character YouTube video IDs** from the video URLs, put them in \`videos.musicId\` and \`videos.interviewId\`. Video players appear in a modal.
5. Open \`index.html\` in a browser or deploy the *copy* as a **separate static site** to Render. Do not reuse or overwrite the G5IVE production/preview service.

## Design retained
- Major-label-inspired black/white/red editorial branding, strong display typography (no cursive).
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts.
- Hero image and copy separated on mobile so jewelry remains visible.
- Optional subtle animated orbital accent; **no floating fake diamond sparkles**.
- Neighborhood story, featured music, interview, two YouTube players, bookings and social links.
- Black/white palette with configurable accent color.

## Client kickoff checklist
- Artist/crew/stage name, city and roots/neighborhood.
- High-quality hero, latest release, interview and community photos.
- Music channel, two YouTube links and Instagram.
- Booking email and any management/social handles.
- Artist approval before publishing or linking to domain.
